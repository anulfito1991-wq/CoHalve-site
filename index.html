Features

Everything co-parents need

From logging a receipt to exporting a 12-month report for your attorney — CoHalve handles it.

📊

Running balance

Always know who owes what. The live balance updates the moment you log an expense.

📄

Court-ready PDF reports

Export dated, itemized expense reports for any time range. Print or share directly from the app.

📷

Receipt scanning

Snap a photo of your receipt — CoHalve reads the amount via OCR and pre-fills the form.

🔔

Reimbursement tracking

Request reimbursement in one swipe. Get a nudge if it hasn't been settled in 3 days.

🔒

Tamper-proof entries

Lock an expense once the co-parent acknowledges it. Locked entries can't be edited or deleted.

⚖️

Flexible split

Set a default split — 50/50, 60/40, or anything you agree on — and override per expense.

👶

Per-child tracking

Tag each expense to a specific child. Filter your history or report by child in one tap.

🏷️

Smart categories

Medical, education, activities, clothing, childcare, travel, and more — built-in from day one.

How it works

Start in under a minute

No accounts. No bank linking. Just open the app and go.

1

Log an expense

Enter the amount, category, and who paid. Snap the receipt if you have it.

2

Request reimbursement

Swipe to request. CoHalve updates the balance and sends a reminder if needed.

3

Mark as paid

When the money moves, mark it settled. The balance adjusts automatically.

4

Export a report

Tap Export. Get a clean PDF with every expense, date, split, and status — ready for court or mediation.
